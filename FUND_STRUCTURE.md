### Management Fees & Carry

**Management Fee**: 2.0% per annum on committed capital
- $5M × 2% = **$100,000/year**
- Covers: Operations, deal sourcing, portfolio management, compliance

**Performance Fee** (Carry): 20% of profits above 8% preferred return

- **Catch-up**: 50% (GP catches up to 20% after hurdle)
